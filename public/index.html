Daily Login / Logout Log

Every employee logs in (Punch In) and out (Punch Out) once per working day. A day with no record means they never logged in — used to flag absence and to adjust productivity against actual shift hours. If someone punches out by mistake, use Reopen to unlock their day, or Edit to correct a login or logout time. All times are New Zealand time.
