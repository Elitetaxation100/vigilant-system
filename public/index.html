Task Engine — Measuring Output & Quality

Every task moves through allocation, live time-tracking, and quality verification before a single point is awarded. No credit is issued for incomplete or unconfirmed work.

3.0 hrs
Standard GST Filing turnaround  ·  40–45 min fast-track benchmark
📋

Granular Work Allocation

Customizable point weights per task type — every role is measured on the work that actually defines it.

⏱

Live Time Tracking

Daily summaries and TAT benchmarks per role, so managers see exactly where time is going in real time.

✔

Quality Verification

Work must be confirmed by a reviewer before points are awarded — no credit for incomplete submissions.

🔔

Smart Notifications

The moment a task lands in someone's queue, gets paused, or a window is proposed, it surfaces in their bell and banner — no need to dig for it.

🔐

Role-Based Approvals

Only Admins and Superadmins can approve or reject a proposed window — enforced at login, not just hidden in the UI.

📡

Live Activity Sync

Every assignment, approval, pause, and completion is logged as it happens — a real audit trail, not a mockup.
